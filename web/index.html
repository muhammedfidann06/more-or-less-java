📜 Rules

  1. First move: any tile. Its digit becomes Current.
  2. Second move: a tile whose row or column number is divisible by Current.
  3. Then: row divisible by Previous and column by Current – or the other way round.
Glowing tiles are the ones you may pick. Hover to preview the new sum.
